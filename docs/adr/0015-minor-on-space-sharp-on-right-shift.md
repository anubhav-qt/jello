# 0015: Minor on Space, sharp on right Shift

- Status: Superseded by 0020
- Date: 2026-10-20

The chord keys A to G keep their major chords (ADR 0012). Holding Space,
under the thumb, makes them play minor chords: Space + A is A minor.
Holding right Shift raises the root a half step, like typing a capital:
Shift + F is F#, and E and B rise to F and C. Both together play sharp
minor chords, so all 24 major and minor chords are playable. A chord is
decided when its key goes down. Only chord keys change: the melody already
has a key for every half step, so a held Shift never bends it. Left Shift
stays free for layer keys. Details are in
[design.md](../design.md#computer-keyboard).
