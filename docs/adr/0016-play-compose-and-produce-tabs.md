# 0016: Play, Compose and Produce tabs

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-20

The top bar has three tabs: Play (the keyboard), Compose (writing a song's
chords, with guided mode) and Produce (arranging and mixing, later). The
keyboard plays on every tab. Until a tab's screen is designed, it shows a
plainly labeled dev panel above the keyboard.
