# 0020: Every chord on its own key

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-20

Holding Space or right Shift while playing chords was hard to combine, so
all 24 major and minor chords get a key of their own on the left hand, and
no key changes another. The letters A to G keep their major chords
(ADR 0012). Each minor sits next to its major: one key to the left (Cm on
X, Dm on S, Em on W, Bm on V), or one key up where that key is taken (Am
on Q, Fm on R, Gm on T). The black-key majors run low to high on 1 to 5
(C#, Eb, F#, Ab, Bb), and their minors around the left edge: `, Tab, left
Shift, Z and Space (C#m, Ebm, F#m, G#m, Bbm). The wave switch moves to \.
Right Shift is kept for the looper. Supersedes ADR 0015. Details are in
[design.md](../design.md#computer-keyboard).
