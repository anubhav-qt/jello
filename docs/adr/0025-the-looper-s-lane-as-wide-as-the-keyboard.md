# 0025: The looper's lane, as wide as the keyboard

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-21

The lane under the keyboard is as wide as the keyboard's panel, in every
tab. Each layer of the loop gets its own box, side by side: one box fills
the lane, and up to four share its width. From the fifth on, four sit on a
level and the levels share the lane's height. Each box draws its notes as
rows of dots in their note colors, with its own playhead; the layers are
one length, so the playheads move together. Notes are drawn where they
play, snapped or not. The lane's right end says how long the loop is and
holds the snap switch, styled like the Wave button and always shown, so
snap can be set before recording. Messages move from the bottom left to
the top bar, after the title, typed out as before. Right Shift stays as in
ADR 0024. Supersedes ADR 0024's lane: its place, its stacked rows and its
scrolling. Past eight layers is decided when layers are built. Details are
in [design.md](../design.md#looper).
