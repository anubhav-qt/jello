# 0039: Fullscreen guided workspace

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-23

Use the whole window for a scrollable workspace above a compact keyboard and
looper dock. Sound lives left of Wave and works in every tab. Escape closes
Sound or Songs before it can remove a loop layer. This supersedes the centered,
overlaid panel placement in 0010, 0022 and 0033.

Keep song-key outlines and saved-progression rankings from 0017, 0023 and 0034.
Add a live reference that follows every chord press, shared-tone companions,
and next-melody suggestions after every note. Tries remain separate from the
song; selecting history changes the reference, explicit Add and reorder build
the final progression. Playing a chord resumes live following.

Keep the left-chord/right-melody split, expand melody to 24 distinct notes,
and use bare arrows to transpose all future notes and chords: up/down one
octave, right/left two. Left Command plus arrows edits the picked sound value
(left Alt on Windows), replacing plain-arrow editing from 0033. Windows glide
moves to right Alt. F12 replaces backslash for Wave. Held notes release at
the pitches they started; saved chords retain their recorded octave.
